About Me

Hi, I'm Les!

For over 14 years, I've been the go-to person behind the scenes of shopping malls, residential communities, and leasing offices — making sure every lease, rent roll, and compliance document is where it should be.

I spent seven years as an SAP system custodian handling lease and financial data across a 60-mall portfolio. Later, I helped build property operations from scratch for a 217-bed residential community — workflows, dashboards, tracking systems, the works. Somewhere along the way, a CEO also asked for my take on a financial analysis that ended up shaping a real business decision — still one of my proudest work moments.

Today I bring that same hands-on, detail-first approach to remote property teams — the kind of support where things get handled before you even have to ask.

What I Do

What I Take Off Your Plate

Lease documents, tenant records, maintenance requests, reports — on any given week, all of it needs attention at once. That's where I come in, keeping everything where it should be as your portfolio grows.

01
Lease Administration

Never lose track of lease agreements and renewals again.

I keep the full lease lifecycle in order — records, renewal timelines, and documentation — so nothing slips through.

Deadlines tracked ahead of time, records ready for an audit any day.
02
Tenant Management

Keep tenant information easy to find, not scattered.

Instead of details spread across emails and spreadsheets, tenant records live in one place you can actually search.

Information you can find in seconds, not five different tabs.
03
Maintenance Coordination

Stay on top of every maintenance request.

I track requests from first report to resolution, with clear communication the whole way through.

Better visibility, and tenants who feel heard along the way.
04
Rent Tracking

Make rent monitoring simpler and more consistent.

Payments, balances, and follow-ups tracked in one place instead of chased down manually every month.

Cleaner records, far less manual chasing.
05
Reporting & Dashboards

See what's happening across your properties at a glance.

I build dashboards that show occupancy, renewals, and performance clearly, instead of buried in separate files.

Decisions backed by numbers you can actually trust.
06
Process Improvement

Build better systems for smoother daily operations.

The best operations aren't run on hard work alone. I look at what's actually not working and fix the process, not just the symptom.

Fewer bottlenecks that don't depend on someone remembering every step.

None of this is complicated.

It just needs someone paying attention to it every day — that's the part I take off your plate.

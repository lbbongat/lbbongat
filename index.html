Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

Q
What are your rates?
My rates depend on the scope of work, number of hours, and type of support needed. I offer hourly, retainer, and project-based packages. Let's chat so I can give you a custom quote that fits your budget and needs.
Q
What are your working hours?
I'm based in the Philippines (Philippine Standard Time) and can work flexible hours to accommodate clients in different time zones. We'll agree on a schedule during onboarding that works for both of us.
Q
How do we get started?
Simple! Book a free 30-minute discovery call so we can talk about your needs. After that, I'll send a proposal, we sign an agreement, and I'll be ready to start within a few days.
Q
Do you sign NDAs or contracts?
Absolutely. I take confidentiality seriously — especially in real estate where sensitive tenant and financial data is involved. I'm fully open to signing an NDA or service contract before we begin.
Q
How do you communicate with clients?
I'm flexible and will adapt to your preferred communication tool — whether that's email, Slack, WhatsApp, Zoom, or Google Meet. I provide regular updates so you always know where things stand.
Q
Do you only work with real estate clients?
My primary niche is real estate backend operations — specifically lease administration and property management support. That's where I have 13+ years of real, hands-on experience. I'm not a generalist VA; I'm a specialist in the backend side of real estate.
Q
What if I only need help for a short-term project?
No problem! I offer project-based arrangements for one-time tasks like setting up a lease tracker, creating a filing system, or drafting a batch of tenant communication templates.
Q
How do you handle data privacy and security?
I follow strict data handling practices — no sharing of client information with third parties, secure file storage, and access only to what's needed for the task. Your data and your tenants' data are always protected.
Still have questions? I'm happy to answer anything before we get started — no pressure, no commitment required.

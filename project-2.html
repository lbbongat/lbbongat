Featured Project

Tenant Onboarding System

One connected Google Sheets workspace for managing tenants, leases, rent collection, maintenance, and reporting instead of five different files.

Project Context: Adapted from a real property management workflow I developed during my corporate career, recreated here for a U.S./international portfolio audience.

Skills Demonstrated: Tenant Administration • Documentation • Workflow Design • Google Workspace • Process Organization
The Story
🔍
The Problem

Our tenant move-in process was highly manual and took around 45 minutes. Multiple forms, requirement submissions, signatures, a briefing, and payment steps, all handled on the spot. Tenants regularly asked why there were so many forms and why it took so long.

→
🛠️
My Approach

I reviewed the workflow for steps that could happen before move-in day. I built a Google Form for applications so requirements could be submitted and reviewed in advance, then sent approved tenants a welcome email with their lease, house rules, and move-in guide ahead of time, along with supporting move-in/move-out documents to keep the process consistent.

→
✨
The Result

Average move-in time dropped from about 45 minutes to 20. Tenants arrived better prepared, requirements were already reviewed, and the team spent far less time on repetitive paperwork during move-in.

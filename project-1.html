Featured Project

Property Management Operations System

One connected Google Sheets workspace for managing tenants, leases, rent collection, maintenance, and reporting — instead of five different files.

Project Context: Adapted from a real property management workflow I developed during my corporate career, recreated here for a U.S./international portfolio audience.

Skills Demonstrated: Property Management • Systems Thinking • Google Sheets • Dashboard Reporting • Process Improvement
The Story
🔍
The Problem

We didn't have a dedicated CRM for leasing operations, just scattered Excel trackers and manual updates. Renewals, maintenance, and occupancy were hard to track quickly, and departments like Accounting had to request reports from us just to stay current.

→
🛠️
My Approach

I built a connected Google Sheets system that brought our key trackers into one workspace with a centralized dashboard, giving other departments direct access to current information instead of waiting on manual reports.

→
✨
The Result

The team gained a clearer view of daily operations and could act on issues faster. Since we were based away from headquarters, Accounting and Building Administration could check the dashboard directly instead of waiting on us for updates.
